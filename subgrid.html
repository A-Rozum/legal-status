Red Flags: When to Seek Immediate Legal Counsel

Legal and Investigative Actions:

  • Summons from investigators or a court in your home country
  • Home searches
  • Freezing or seizure of personal and business assets
  • News of criminal charges

Pressure and Threats:

  • Threats against you
  • Pressure on you or your relatives to return to your home country
  • Negative media campaigns
  • Negative public statements about you by government officials

Travel and Immigration Issues:

  • Travel restrictions
  • Unexpected difficulties with obtaining or renewing a residency permit
  • Entry refusals
  • Passport cancellation or refusal to renew your passport abroad

Business and Financial Disruptions:

  • Unexpected business audits or investigations by public authorities
  • License issues
  • Bank account closures or refusals to open a new account
  • Increased transaction scrutiny
